# Content confirmation checklist

The replacement site deliberately avoids exact prices and unsupported statistics. Confirm these details with the nursery before the production DNS cutover.

## Places and sessions

- Current accepted starting age
- Exact baby, toddler, and preschool room boundaries
- Current full-day and half-day session patterns
- Whether the stated minimum of three half days remains current
- Exact annual closure dates behind the 51-week claim
- Current availability and waiting-list process

## Fees and funding

- Current fees by age and session
- Registration fee and deposit
- Sibling discount terms
- Consumable, meal, and additional-service charges
- How funded hours are stretched or restricted across 51 weeks
- Whether working-parent funding is accepted from the youngest currently eligible age
- What is and is not included when a funded place is used
- Confirm that the April 2026 Parent Information PDF remains current, especially its fee and funding tables
- Confirm whether the July 2024 Terms and Conditions PDF is the signed-off contract: its source text still contains bracketed template alternatives

## Food and care

- Current on-site chef status
- Current sample menu
- Food hygiene rating
- Allergy and special-diet procedures
- Whether all meals and snacks are included in fees

The nursery website says it offers half days and all-inclusive meals prepared on site. Its daynurseries listing says no half days and no meals. Correct the directory after the current facts are confirmed.

## Team and safeguarding

- Current manager and deputy names
- Current SENCO contact
- Current staffing and qualification summary
- Approved wording for CCTV
- Approved wording for parent-app photographs and privacy
- Consent to reuse manager portraits

## Contact and access

- Choose one canonical phone number. The website uses 01895 603 097, while Ofsted lists 07984 919111.
- Current bus routes at the stop outside
- Current parking capacity and Turnpike Lane access instructions
- Accessibility arrangements for visitors
- Accuracy of walking and driving distances to Brunel University, Uxbridge station, and Hillingdon Hospital

## Claims and third-party marks

- Evidence and time period for the claim that over 90 percent of children receive a first-choice private-school offer
- Current permission to reuse all parent testimonials
- Current NDNA membership before showing a badge, because the downloaded badge is dated 2022 to 2023
- Current permission to show third-party marks such as Ofsted and EyLog
- Ownership or migration rights for the logo, interior photography, staff portraits, and stock images

The implemented site uses the Ofsted badge with a date and a link to the official report. It does not use the dated NDNA badge or the private-school admissions statistic.
